# Music

Put royalty-free tracks here (mp3, wav, m4a). With `music: file: auto` the renderer uses an episode's
`audio\music.*` first, otherwise the first track in this folder (alphabetical), loops it, and dips it
under the narration.

For the reference-Reel feel, pick a light, curious or suspenseful instrumental (no vocals), around
90–120 BPM. Free sources:
- Pixabay Music (pixabay.com/music): free for commercial social videos, no attribution needed. Avoid tracks
  with the shield icon (registered with Content ID) and keep the download page link in case of a claim.
- Instagram's own music library: add it inside the Instagram app when posting instead (set `music: file: null`).

Never use songs you don't have a licence for.
