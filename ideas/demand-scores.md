How many people already care about each topic, from three public signals (pulled in the browser, 2026-10-25):

- **Wikipedia**: average monthly views of the topic's English article, Oct 2025 – Sep 2026 (topic curiosity).
- **YouTube**: views of the top video and the median of the first 10 results for a plain search on the topic (proven video demand).
- **Google Trends** (US, 12 months, "Mary Celeste" as the shared anchor): used only as a direction check because many terms are ambiguous (Centralia is also a city; Pringles and quicksand are generic).

**Demand score (0–10)** = 35 % Wikipedia + 65 % YouTube, both on a log scale. "Mine" = the creative score out of 40 (hook, payoff, Easter egg, viral fit).

## Reading it
- **Top demand + strong story:** the snap, the Wow! signal, the lion hug, the Gimli Glider.
- **Huge demand but crowded:** Snake Island and Centralia (MrBeast-scale videos and 20M+ Shorts already exist); only worth it with a fresh angle.
- **Rising now:** the Voynich manuscript (Google Trends: the last 8 weeks are well above its 12-month average).
- **Low demand, drop or park:** Pringles, kidney chain, astronauts taller, airplane food.
- The penguin scores low only because its Wikipedia page is tiny; its YouTube demand (13.7M) is real.

## Caveats
- These measure interest in the topic, not how our version will do; 8 posts are too few to check the link yet.
- The snap row is inflated by Marvel interest (the Thanos page and fan videos), which is exactly the trend we want to ride, but our video must not show the characters.
- YouTube search results are personalised a little and the top hit can be an unrelated viral video; the median is the steadier number.
