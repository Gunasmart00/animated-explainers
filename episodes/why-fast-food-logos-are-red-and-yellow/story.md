# Why Fast Food Logos Are Red and Yellow

The "Ketchup and Mustard Theory" in fast-food marketing and color psychology.

## Core Facts & Verified Sources

1. **Claim:** The combination of red and yellow in fast-food branding is known in marketing as the "Ketchup and Mustard Theory."
   - Source 1: Business Insider (https://www.businessinsider.com/fast-food-red-and-yellow-logos-ketchup-mustard-theory-2018-8)
   - Source 2: Colour Studies / Karen Haller Applied Colour Psychology (https://www.colourstudies.com)

2. **Claim:** Red stimulates physical excitement, increases heart rate, and triggers appetite and impulsive cravings.
   - Source 1: Graphic Design Blog / Psychology of Color in Marketing (https://graphicdesignblog.org)
   - Source 2: University of New Hampshire / Behavioral Science Research on Color Stimulation

3. **Claim:** Yellow is scientifically the most visible color in daylight and from long distances (roadside signs/highways), and stimulates feelings of comfort and optimism.
   - Source 1: Karen Haller Color Psychology (https://karenhaller.com)
   - Source 2: Business Insider Architecture & Branding Analysis

4. **Claim:** Warm colors create urgency, prompting fast eating and high customer turnover (table turnover).
   - Source 1: EconLife Consumer Psychology (https://econlife.com)
   - Source 2: AI Logo Creator / Fast Food Design Psychology (https://ailogocreator.io)

5. **Claim:** Blue is an evolutionary appetite suppressant because blue naturally occurring food is rare and often signals poison or spoilage.
   - Source 1: Psychology Today (https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/sensory-marketing/why-blue-suppresses-appetite)
   - Source 2: WebMD / Food Science

6. **Claim:** Brands like Subway and Starbucks deliberately use green to project health, freshness, and relaxation so customers linger and feel guilt-free.
   - Source 1: Karen Haller Branding Case Studies (https://karenhaller.com)
   - Source 2: Brand To Life Visual Merchandising (https://brandstolife.com.au)
