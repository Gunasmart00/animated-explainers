Owner brief (2026-10-20): idea #1 from the pitch. Space, "oh that's genius". Accurate visuals from NASA references.

## 2. Visual facts (geometry the prompts must keep true)
- **Galileo spacecraft** (refs 01, 02, 03):
  - an octagonal main body wrapped in gold and black thermal blankets;
  - on top, a large white **umbrella-like mesh dish, 4.8 m across, that is only partly open**: lopsided, with about 3 of its 18 ribs stuck to the central mast (the refs show it fully open; ours must be jammed);
  - two long booms, each with a cylindrical radioisotope generator (RTG) about 5 m out;
  - one very long thin 11 m magnetometer boom with small sensor pods.
  - About 6 m tall overall: a tiny speck next to Jupiter. **No text or logos on it.**
- **Jupiter** (ref 04): cream, tan, orange and brown cloud bands, the Great Red Spot, slightly flattened at the poles. Jupiter is about 11 Earths wide: always enormous next to anything man-made.
- **Europa** (refs 05, 06):
  - a smooth white-to-tan ice ball criss-crossed by long reddish-brown crack lines;
  - a bit smaller than Earth's Moon;
  - interior cutaway: an ice shell (3–30 km) over a deep dark-blue ocean (about 100 km deep) over a rocky interior.
- **Cassini** (refs 07, 08):
  - a tall cylindrical body (about 6.8 m) wrapped in gold foil;
  - a big white 4 m dish antenna on top;
  - a long thin magnetometer boom.
- **Saturn** (ref 10): pale gold bands and huge flat rings.
- **Enceladus** (ref 09): a small bright-white icy moon with tall feathery plumes of ice spray rising from its south pole.
- **Mission control:** a bright, modern, generic control room, not a real NASA room. It has:
  - rows of consoles and one giant curved wall screen;
  - no readable text, numbers or logos on screens or clothes (the build adds pop-ups).
- **Germ hitchhikers (Easter egg):**
  - three tiny, cute, pale-green rounded cartoon microbes with little smiling faces, clinging to Galileo's gold foil;
  - only noticeable on close-ups in s02–s03, then revealed in s08.
