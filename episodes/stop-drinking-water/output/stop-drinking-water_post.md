## 1. Fact check (2 reliable sources per claim, before posting)
| Claim | Sources |
|---|---|
| An adult body is about 55-65% water | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK555956/ · https://www.usgs.gov/special-topic/water-science-school/science/water-you |
| Blood gets more concentrated as you lose water; sensors in the hypothalamus detect it and trigger thirst | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK555956/ · https://www.msdmanuals.com/home/kidney-disorders/water-balance/dehydration |
| Thirst doesn't appear until you're about 1-2% dehydrated | https://today.uconn.edu/2012/02/even-mild-dehydration-can-alter-mood/ · https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK555956/ |
| The brain releases vasopressin (ADH), which makes the kidneys hold on to water, so urine gets darker and scarcer | https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/23150-posterior-pituitary · https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/9013-dehydration |
| Mild dehydration brings headaches, tiredness and trouble concentrating | https://today.uconn.edu/2012/02/even-mild-dehydration-can-alter-mood/ · https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/21517-dehydration-headache |
| Mild dehydration lowers mood, and in the study the mood changes were substantially greater in women than in men | https://today.uconn.edu/2012/02/even-mild-dehydration-can-alter-mood/ · https://www.painscience.com/biblio/mild-dehydration-affects-mood-in-healthy-young-women.html |
| Blood volume falls, the heart beats faster, standing up can make you dizzy | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK555956/ · https://www.msdmanuals.com/home/kidney-disorders/water-balance/dehydration |
| You sweat less and can overheat | https://www.msdmanuals.com/home/kidney-disorders/water-balance/dehydration · https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/9013-dehydration |
| Water is pulled out of cells, including brain cells; confusion is a sign of severe dehydration | https://www.msdmanuals.com/home/kidney-disorders/water-balance/dehydration · https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK555956/ |
| With less blood reaching them, the kidneys can be injured and fail | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK555956/ · https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/325174 |
| Most people survive only about 3 days without water, less in heat | https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/325174 · https://www.healthline.com/health/food-nutrition/how-long-can-you-live-without-water |
