# A Day on Venus Is Longer Than Its Year

Owner pitch #4 (2026-10-21): space, imaginative story. Hook = wide golden Venus zooming to a kid astronaut with a
birthday cake, confused that the sun hasn't moved. Twist = the sun rises in the west. Easter egg = the sunrise on the
"wrong" side of a compass. Rules: `docs/owner-creative-rules.md`.

### Correction to the pitch
"You'd have a birthday before your first sunset" is **not true**. Sunrise to sunrise is ~117 Earth days, so sunset
comes ~58 days after sunrise, long before a 225-day birthday. What *is* true and just as surprising:
- one **spin** (243 d) is longer than the **year** (225 d), and
- a whole Venus year has **fewer than two sunrises** (225 / 117 ≈ 1.9).
The climax uses that instead.

### Story setting (imaginary, labelled as such in the caption)
The kid lives in a floating cloud city above Venus's yellow clouds (based on NASA's HAVOC concept), so the Sun is
visible and nobody is in danger on the 465 °C surface. Never sad: cosy, curious, funny.

## 2. Narration (for review, ~104 words, ~43 s at Mark's pace)
1. On Venus, one day is longer than a whole year.
2. Meet Ava. She lives in a floating city above the clouds of Venus, and today is her birthday.
3. There's just one problem: the sun has barely moved since breakfast.
4. Venus spins so slowly that one full turn takes 243 Earth days.
5. But it zooms around the Sun in just 225.
6. So her whole year is over before Venus finishes a single spin.
7. Even sunrise to sunrise takes 117 Earth days.
8. So Ava gets fewer than two sunrises per birthday.
9. Oh, and when the sun finally does rise...
10. it rises in the west, because Venus spins backwards.

Loop: "...because Venus spins backwards." runs into "On Venus, one day is longer than a whole year."

## 3. Shot plan (one continuous camera journey, 6 clips, 6 keyframes; prompts written after the voice)
- **Hook (0–4 s):** wide golden-yellow Venus from space, fast zoom down through the clouds to a glass-domed floating
  city; inside, Ava (about 8, cute stylised 3D, small spacesuit with a bubble helmet off) holds a birthday cake and
  frowns at the sun, puzzled. Emotion by 3 s.
- **Easter egg:** a big brass compass on the dome wall: its red needle points up (north) and the sun is on the LEFT
  (west). Pays off in the twist. (No letters on it: Flow garbles text.)
- **Build-up:** cutaway of Venus spinning slowly like a lazy top beside a clock; Venus racing around the Sun with an
  orbit trail; a year-ring completes before the spin-ring.
- **Climax:** a calendar of sunrises: Ava's birthday party with only one-and-a-bit suns on the wall.
- **Twist (last second):** the sun peeks over the horizon on the compass's west side; Ava's jaw drops; loop back to
  the wide Venus shot.
