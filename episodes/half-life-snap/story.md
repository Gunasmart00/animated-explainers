# half-life-snap: research and narration

**Idea (owner pick 2026-10-25):** "What if half of all life vanished in a snap?" Fictional story-telling built on real
facts. Never name the Marvel character or show a gauntlet: our recurring **Everyman** snaps his fingers.
Lane: what-happens-if / curiosity science. Mood: curious and surprising, never sad (nobody is shown hurt; people
simply vanish mid-step, the story is about what the planet and his body do next).

## Facts and sources (2 each)
| Claim in the narration | Sources |
|---|---|

**Honesty notes**
- The snap is fiction; every number in the narration is real and comes from a real event that "previews" it.
- 2020 seismic drop = "up to 50%" (the biggest drops, in cities), not everywhere.
- Gut recovery after antibiotics is "mostly": a few species didn't return in the study. Narration says "most of them".
- Whale study = North Atlantic right whales, Bay of Fundy, after 11 Sept 2001. Narration says "in 2001" only (no tragedy on screen).

## Narration (for Mark) — 108 words, ≈38 s
He snapped his fingers... and half of everything alive on Earth vanished.
Half the people on his street, half the birds, half the trees.
But then, something strange happened. The whole planet got quieter.
You see, when the world stayed home in 2020, the shaking in the ground dropped by up to fifty percent.
And when ship noise fell in 2001, whales' stress hormones dropped too.
So wild animals moved in, like the puma that walked through Santiago... and the goats that took over a town in Wales.
But the snap took half of something else.
Nineteen trillion bacteria living inside him.
Well... in about six weeks, most of them grew back.
Even the ones on the fingers that snapped.

**Loop:** "...on the fingers that snapped." runs straight into "He snapped his fingers..." (last frame = his fingers
mid-snap, the first frame of S1).

## Story shape (owner rules)
- **Hook (0-1 s):** the snap itself, mid-action. **Emotion by 3 s:** his shocked face as half the crowd blinks out.
- **Opening:** wide establishing shot of the whole street, camera already zooming in.
- **Progression:** quieter planet → whales → animals in cities. No payoff early.
- **Climax:** the snap took half of HIS bacteria too (the topic answered at body scale).
- **Twist (last second):** even the bacteria on the fingers that snapped → loop.
- **Easter egg:** a small orange hand-sanitiser bottle clipped to his belt loop in the first frame ("why is that
  there?"); paid off by the bacteria twist.
