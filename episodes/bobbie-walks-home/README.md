## Transcript (draft for the owner to record)
A skinny dog limped down Main Street, his toenails worn down to almost nothing, when a girl outside her family's
diner froze. It was Bobbie.
Six months earlier, on a road trip to Indiana, three dogs had chased him away from a gas station.
The family searched for days, then drove all the way home to Oregon without him.
But Bobbie never gave up. He crossed plains and deserts, swam rivers, and climbed the Rocky Mountains in the middle
of winter. 2,551 miles.
And when his story hit the newspapers, letters poured in from strangers who had fed him along the way.
They used them to map his whole journey home.
