Owner's brief (2026-10-20): Explain the dual-locking mechanical secret inside a seatbelt retractor. 
Hook: If you pull a seatbelt gently, it rolls smoothly... but yank it fast, and it instantly jams. 
Show the internal B-pillar cutaway, the two independent mechanical triggers (the swinging pendulum and the centrifugal clutch), and the pyrotechnic pre-tensioner.

## 2. Checked but NOT Used

- **"Seatbelts are triggered by electrical magnets in normal braking":** Debunked. Standard retractor locking is 100% mechanical. It functions even if the car's battery is completely dead or disconnected. (Pyrotechnic pretensioners use an electric pulse, but the primary mechanical lock is fully analog).
- **Exact decibel level of the retractor click:** Not referenced as decibel ratings vary widely across vehicle manufacturers.
- **Specific G-force thresholds ($0.7\text{ g}$ vs $1.2\text{ g}$):** Kept conceptual rather than bombarding viewers with fractional numbers; focus is on the tangible contrast between "slow pull" and "rapid yank".
