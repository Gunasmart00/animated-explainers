**Summary:** In 1995 a woman in Mora, Sweden lost her wedding ring while doing her Christmas baking. Sixteen years later,
pulling the last carrots of the season, she found it wrapped around a tiny carrot. Twist: it didn't fit anymore.

## Characters, objects, and locations
- **Locations:** a red wooden Swedish house with a vegetable patch in autumn (2011); the family kitchen at Christmas (1995).
- **Characters:** Lena (about 50 in 2011; about 34 in 1995), her husband Ola, their daughter Anna (who heard the scream).
  Looks are invented (we never copy real people's faces).
- **Objects:** a white-gold wedding ring with seven small diamonds (she designed it); a small crooked carrot; a compost
  bin; a bowl of vegetable peelings; Christmas cookies.

## Transcript (draft, for the owner to record as Mark)
A woman almost threw away a tiny carrot from her garden... then she saw what was wrapped around it. Her wedding ring.
Sixteen years earlier, she'd set it down while doing her Christmas baking... and it vanished.
She searched everywhere. Years later, they even pulled up the kitchen floor. Nothing.
You see, it had probably fallen in with the vegetable peelings, gone into the compost, and been spread over the garden.
Then a single carrot seed landed right in the middle of it... and grew straight through.
Finally, she had her ring back.
But after sixteen years... it didn't fit anymore.

Loop: "...it didn't fit anymore." runs into "A woman almost threw away a tiny carrot...".

## Trivia
- Based on a true story: Lena Påhlsson, Mora, Dalarna, Sweden; lost 1995, found October 2011.
- **Easter egg:** a carrot sits in the vegetable bowl on the 1995 kitchen counter, right next to the ring.
- **Twist:** her fingers had grown in 16 years; the ring had to be resized.
- Not used: her husband's theory that the family sheep ate it (funny, but unconfirmed; could be a pinned-comment question).
