# Zack reference library (private, `refs/zack/`, git-ignored)

Downloaded by Antigravity on 2026-10-24 from the fan wiki: 93 images (87 from character pages, 6 from short pages), index in `refs/zack/index.csv`.
Curated by Claude into `refs/zack/curated/` (captions and branding cropped out) with `catalog.yaml`. `tools/gen_image.py` adds
them to every image job automatically as **style references only** (`style:` in images.yaml).

Rules: style only; never reproduce his characters (named or recurring), his persona, logos or captions; Claude checks every
output for lookalikes before it is used; these files are never committed, posted or used as Flow video inputs.

## Not used (and why)
- **His persona and recurring named characters** (the headband guy, Alex, Bike Girl, the Ensemble): too identifiable.
- **Real photos and real people** (Zack IRL, merch, greenscreen, the puppet, the real hippo, the city aerial, the paintings).
- **Logos, banners, thumbnails with his "D" logo or text** (shirts or shells saying ZACK D FILMS, the newspaper, Discord screenshot, question-mark icon).
- **Gore or injury** (tortured guy, hippo attack, crocodile eating, bruised face).
- **Broken files** (heavily dithered or cut-out images: scarab, elephants #19/#20, hippo #24, sauropod, narwhal/whale cut-outs).
- The originals were deleted on 2026-10-24 (owner): only `curated/` and `index.csv` are kept.
