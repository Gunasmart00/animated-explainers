- **Post when the US is awake.** Schedule each Short for **7 PM US Eastern**:

  Use Studio's **Schedule** option so nobody has to be awake.
- **Country of residence stays India.** It's a legal and tax setting for payments, it must be true, and it doesn't limit who sees the videos.
- **Pace:** one Short a day at most. Steady and researched beats volume (see the risk below).

### Money, honestly
- **Partner Program today:** 1,000 subscribers plus **10 M Shorts views in 90 days** (or 4,000 watch hours).
- **From 1 Feb 2027:** new applicants need **20 M Shorts views in 90 days** (or 8,000 hours). Shorts ad revenue then needs 10 M views per 90 days for everyone. If we qualify before 31 Jan 2027, apply before then. ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-ad-eligibility-requirements-shorts/))
- **The real risk is YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy.** It covers mass-produced, templated or AI videos made with no creative input. AI-narration Shorts farms were removed from the Partner Program in early 2026. ([AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/monetization/youtube-monetization-policy-changes-2026-a-complete-dated-timeline))

  What protects us is what we already do: an original researched script with sources for every video, consistent characters, a hand-checked edit, no near-duplicates, and no daily flood. Keep the sources in every description so a reviewer can see the work.

## 2. Create the channel (you do this part, about 10 minutes)

I can't create accounts for you, so these steps are yours.

1. **Open the channel creator.** On a computer, sign in to YouTube with the Google account that should own HowZit, then go to **youtube.com/channel_switcher → Create a channel**. This makes a Brand Account, which:
   - lets the channel have its own name;
   - lets you add a manager later (for example a Lowkey Genius account) without sharing your password.
2. **Name:** `HowZit`.
3. **Handle:** `@howzit.explains`, to match Instagram. If it's taken, try `@howzitexplains`, then `@howzit_explains`.
4. **Verify the phone:** studio.youtube.com → Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility. This unlocks custom thumbnails and longer videos.
5. **Customisation** (studio.youtube.com → Customisation):
   - **Profile picture:** `HowZit - YouTube profile picture.png`. It's the Z? bubble, which survives YouTube's circle crop.
   - **Banner:** `HowZit - YouTube banner 2560x1440.png`. The logo and tagline sit inside the area every device shows; the side cards only show on desktop and TV.
   - **Description:** paste the text below.
   - **Links:** add your Instagram, `https://instagram.com/howzit.explains`.
   - **Contact email:** a business email, not your personal one.
6. **Settings → Channel:**
   - **Basic info:** Country = India. Keywords: `how things work, science explained, 3d animation, explained in 60 seconds, unsolved mysteries, human body facts, history facts`.
   - **Advanced → Audience:** "No, set this channel as not made for kids". This is honest for us: the topics (hijackings, dehydration, exam cheating) are aimed at teens and adults. The cartoon style doesn't change that. If a future video is aimed at children, mark that video "made for kids".
7. **Settings → Upload defaults:**
   - Category: **Education**
   - Video language: **English**
   - Comments: **on**
   - Altered content: **Yes**. This matches our Instagram rule of always disclosing the AI.

## 3. Uploading the three Shorts

### Rules for every upload
- **Title:** keywords first, no fake claims, under 100 characters.
- **Hashtags:** 3 to 5, at the end of the description, including `#shorts`.
  - YouTube shows up to 3 of them above the title.
  - Over 60 hashtags makes YouTube ignore all of them.
  - Unrelated hashtags (Marvel, Taylor Swift…) can get a video removed under the misleading-metadata policy. ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658))
- **Tags** (Show more → Tags): 5 to 8 phrases, used mainly to catch misspellings. YouTube says tags "play a minimal role" in discovery; the title and the first seconds do the work. ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402))
- **Altered or synthetic content: Yes.**
  - YouTube requires the label for realistic AI and puts it in the description for animated videos.
  - Veo videos may get a permanent label automatically anyway.
  - The label doesn't affect reach or money. ([YouTube blog, 27 May 2026](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/improving-ai-labels-viewers-creators/))
- **Audience:** No, not made for kids. **Category:** Education.
- **Thumbnail:** Shorts show a frame from the video. In the YouTube app, pick the cover frame. If Studio offers a thumbnail upload, use the cover jpg where there is one.
- **Pin the comment** below each video as soon as it's live.
- **Playlists:** "Unsolved Mysteries" (D.B. Cooper), "Your Body, Explained" (Water), "Life Hacks & School" (Gen Z Teacher). Playlists help people binge.

### Upload order
- **Day 1:** D.B. Cooper. It has the strongest curiosity hook and is the most Zack-like.
- **Day 2:** Stop Drinking Water.
- **Day 3:** Gen Z Teacher.

Upload all three at once and schedule them a day apart at 7 PM ET.
